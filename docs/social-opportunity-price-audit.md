# Five social pages: live-site price audit

Checked 28 October 2026 against the public Cypher Digital packages, reel creation, Facebook Ads and Contact pages. No package prices or existing service pages were changed.

## Calculator correction — 31 October 2026

Rechecked the live packages page at the owner's request. Package fees cover campaign setup/management within package scope; the LKR 25,000 fee is a standalone-service starting price. Removed that automatic surcharge from the calculator only. Default Basic + LKR 50,000 Meta budget is now LKR 135,000; café + LKR 40,000 is LKR 125,000; Silver + LKR 50,000 is LKR 150,000. Updated examples and WhatsApp breakdown. Original audit notes below describe the previous calculation, superseded by this correction. Existing package and other service pages are unchanged.

## Confirmed live prices

- Basic: LKR 85,000/month; 12 posts and three stock-footage reels.
- Silver: LKR 100,000/month; 15 posts, three reels and a four-hour location shoot.
- Gold: LKR 150,000/month; 20 posts, location-shot reels, LinkedIn and two email campaigns. The live package does not specify a reel count.
- Platinum: LKR 250,000/month; unlimited posts, eight reels, an eight-hour shoot, four managed platforms and four email campaigns.
- Packages page advertises standalone Facebook Ads management from LKR 25,000/month. The Facebook Ads page separately describes LKR 25,000/month as a possible starting ad spend; these are different costs, not interchangeable prices.
- Reel creation advertises professional video shoots at LKR 25,000 (one minute), LKR 35,000 (one to three minutes), and LKR 40,000 onwards (five minutes or longer). AI character videos: LKR 18,000 / 30,000 / 45,000 for 15–30 / 60 / 90 seconds.
- Reel travel: Colombo no travel charge; Greater Colombo LKR 6,000; regional LKR 12,000; outstation quoted per trip.

## Clarifications applied only to the five new pages

- Retained all four matching package prices.
- Distinguished the uploaded calculator's LKR 30,000 professional extra-reel allowance from format-specific standalone video prices.
- Identified three Gold reels as a calculator assumption, not a published package entitlement.
- Preserved the supplied calculator arithmetic: LKR 160,000 default, LKR 150,000 cafe example, LKR 175,000 salon example and LKR 150,000 B2B example.
- Marked optional standalone management as an estimate and asked customers to confirm existing campaign inclusions to avoid charging twice for the same scope.
- Kept one-time support and boosting fees quote-based; no actual fee is available on the live site.
- Kept LKR 40,000–60,000 Meta spend as planning guidance, not a compulsory minimum or outcome promise.
- Clarified travel and taxes are not calculated, and actual payment dates, advance and method require a written proposal.
- Corrected the malformed Colombo address answer. Live Contact publishes Colombo, Sri Lanka and Monday–Saturday, 9AM–6PM, but no street address; meetings require confirmation.
- Removed fixed launch/result expectations where evidence is unavailable.
- Kept all five social contact actions on 94760317477.

The packages page's shorthand reel add-on wording differs from the format-specific Reel Creation page.
