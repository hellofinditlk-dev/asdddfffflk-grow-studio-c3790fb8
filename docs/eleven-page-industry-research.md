## Facts incorporated

- DataReportal Digital 2026 Sri Lanka: late-2025 potential ad reach of YouTube 8.82 million, Instagram 2.25 million, TikTok 6.79 million adults 18+. These are ad-tool estimates, not active-user counts. Source: https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sri-lanka
- TikTok self-serve Business Center country list excludes Sri Lanka. Registration, managed accounts, placements and target-country availability are distinct; paid Sri Lankan TikTok-feed delivery is not promised. Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/available-countries-and-regions-for-ad-account-creation-in-bc
- TikTok July 2026 daily budget guidance: campaign budget must exceed US$50, ad-group daily budget must exceed US$20. Lifetime ad-group example: US$20 × 31 days = US$620. Nested thresholds are not separate fees. Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget?lang=en
- Google Shopping/free-listing supported target-country list does not include Sri Lanka. Overseas destinations require separate eligibility checks. Source: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12472394?hl=en
- Google feed review guidance varies: initial target-country submission up to 3 business days; Shopping product review 3–5 business days. Neither is a guaranteed deadline. Sources: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/188477?hl=en and https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12488713?hl=en
- Direct live Google data-segment guidance now states 100 active users in the preceding 30 days for Display, Search and YouTube. This supersedes older commonly repeated 1,000-user Search/YouTube guidance. Older unrefreshed Customer Match lists have a separate legacy exception. Source: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2472738?hl=en
- AdSense lists Sri Lanka as supported; country payment table shows cheque/wire, not EFT/Hyperwallet. USD-account threshold is US$100, subject to holds and verification. Sources: https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13402307?hl=en, https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1714397?hl=en and https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1709871?hl=en
- WhatsApp Platform per-delivered-template-message pricing replaces conversation pricing; service-window replies and utility-template exceptions are described separately. Provider fees may be additional. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing
- Google Ads read-only access can view billing information but not edit campaigns/billing. Corrected the audit page's contrary claim. Source: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9978556?hl=en
- Google Ads individual certifications last one year; no Cypher certification is inferred. Source: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9702955?hl=en
- Web Vitals published good thresholds: LCP 2.5 seconds, INP 200 ms, CLS 0.1 at the 75th percentile. Targets, not promised website outcomes. Source: https://web.dev/articles/vitals

## Advertising cost evidence

No transparent Sri Lanka-wide or industry-by-industry CPC/CPV/CPM/CPL dataset was established. Local provider estimates without disclosed sampling were not represented as independently verified market data. WordStream/LocaliQ's 2025 US benchmark of US$5.26 average CPC is explicitly labelled US, combining Google and Microsoft campaigns, April 2024–March 2025, more than 16,000 campaigns. It is not converted into local prices. Source: https://www.wordstream.com/blog/2025-google-ads-benchmarks

Unverifiable local price gaps are replaced with forecast guidance. LKR 100 CPC budget arithmetic is prominently marked as an assumed educational example, not industry data or a Cypher recommendation. The Google 30.4-times monthly budget limit is sourced separately: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637?hl=en
