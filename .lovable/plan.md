# Phase 2: Facebook authority cluster

## Audit decision
Build the six requested topics as a mix of strengthened existing pages and new gap pages, rather than creating six competing URLs.

- **Facebook Marketing Strategy:** expand `/blog/facebook-campaign-strategy-guide-sri-lankan-brands` into the full strategy article; keep its established URL.
- **Facebook Advertising Cost:** create the focused commercial page `/facebook-ads-cost-sri-lanka` because no current page owns Facebook-only cost intent. Keep agency fees and ad spend clearly separate and use only already confirmed prices.
- **Facebook Lead Generation:** strengthen `/social-media-lead-generation-sri-lanka`, which already explicitly targets Facebook and WhatsApp leads; a second Facebook-only URL would compete with it.
- **Facebook Retargeting:** create `/facebook-retargeting-sri-lanka` as the commercial service page. Keep `/blog/remarketing-retargeting-ads-sri-lanka` as the broader educational guide and link it to the new page.
- **Facebook Ad Creative:** strengthen the existing commercial `/facebook-ad-design-sri-lanka` page for the service intent, and link it to the educational Facebook articles. Do not create a duplicate article unless later search data shows a separate informational gap.
- **Facebook Page Management:** create `/facebook-page-management-sri-lanka` for commercial service intent. Keep `/blog/manage-business-facebook-page-sri-lanka` as the educational guide and link it to the new page.

## Content and SEO work
- Bring each new or substantially updated page to at least 2,000 useful words with a direct answer, practical Sri Lankan examples, tables/checklists, mistakes, action plan, genuine FAQ, mid-page CTA, final CTA, and related reading. The strategy article also receives author and updated-date details.
- Keep claims factual and conservative. Remove or avoid unsupported statistics, guarantees, ratings, client counts, and invented outcomes.
- Give every updated/new article a unique title, description, H1, self-referencing canonical, breadcrumbs, Article schema, and FAQPage schema only where the visible FAQ matches it.
- Use the existing Facebook Ad Design page for commercial creative intent and improve its copy only enough to connect it clearly to campaign strategy and the Facebook Ads hub.

## Internal-link architecture
- Treat `/social-media-management-sri-lanka` as the protected main pillar and `/facebook-ads-sri-lanka` as the Facebook commercial hub.
- Add a compact Facebook guides section to both hubs linking to all six Phase 2 topics.
- Every Phase 2 article links back to the protected pillar, the Facebook Ads hub, and 3–5 relevant commercial pages using varied natural anchor text.
- Cross-link the six topics where the relationship is useful: strategy → cost/lead generation/creative; lead generation → creative/retargeting; page management → strategy/creative; retargeting → lead generation/cost.
- Keep existing URLs unchanged and add no redirects.

## Technical wiring and verification
- Register the three genuinely new commercial routes and include them in the sitemap, live-site routing rules, and server-rendered metadata map. Existing educational articles keep their current URLs.
- Update the SEO architecture report with intent ownership, actions, internal links, and cannibalisation notes.
- Verify desktop and mobile rendering, one H1, canonical and schemas, working links and CTAs, no console errors, and a clean build.
