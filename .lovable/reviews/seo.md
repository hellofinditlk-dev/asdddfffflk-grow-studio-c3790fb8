## Event-page checks

- **Intent fits:** the page targets customers looking for event planning and event management in Sri Lanka, with service formats, coordination responsibilities, quotations and contact actions. General “event planning” searches also include informational intent; the practical guide supports that without creating a duplicate page.
- **Title:** Event Planning & Event Management Sri Lanka | Cypher Digital — 60 characters; both subjects are covered naturally, with Sri Lanka qualifying the combined service.
- **Description:** Event planning and event management in Sri Lanka by Cypher Digital. Corporate events, product launches and brand activations. Request your custom event quote. — 158 characters; relevant and truthful, without ranking or price promises.
- **Verified in the running preview:** one H1, self-referencing no-slash canonical, matching social metadata, loaded concept image, 3,253 rendered page words, working quote jump, working WhatsApp contact dialog and no horizontal overflow at 603px and 390px.
- **Schema:** Service with Organization provider, BreadcrumbList and existing FAQPage; no invented rating or fixed price. Keep FAQ answers aligned with visible content; schema does not guarantee rich results.
- **Discovery:** the event URL is listed in the sitemap and llms.txt. The sitemap still shows 11 November although significant page changes were made on 19 November; a page-specific date correction is appropriate, not a build-time date refresh.
- **Evidence limits:** real photos, team details, case studies and testimonials remain pending. The illustrative venue is labelled, not presented as delivered work. Weather advice should be sourced or softened; “Trusted Partner” and expert positioning would be stronger with owner-supplied evidence.
