# How to Pay for Facebook and Instagram Ads in Sri Lanka

Your ads are ready, your audience is set, and then Meta shows "payment failed". It's one of the most common problems Sri Lankan businesses face with Facebook and Instagram ads. This guide explains how Meta ads billing works, which payment options Sri Lankan advertisers can use, how local billing and agencies fit in, and how to fix declined payments.

## How Meta Ads Billing Works

Facebook and Instagram ads are paid through **Meta Ads Manager**. Each ad account has:

- **A currency**, chosen when the account is created (it can't be changed later without a new ad account).
- **A billing country**, based on your business address.
- **A payment method**, such as a card or prepaid funds, where available.
- **A billing type:** either automatic payments (charged after you spend or reach a threshold) or manual/prepaid payments (you add funds first). Availability depends on your country and currency. [CONFIRM which billing types are available for Sri Lanka]

## Why Your Card Gets Declined – and How to Fix It

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| International online payments are blocked | Ask your bank to enable online and foreign-currency transactions |
| Foreign-currency spending limit reached | Ask your bank about your annual or monthly limit |
| Card details or address don't match | Make sure billing name and address match the bank's records |
| Too many failed attempts | Wait, then retry once with the bank's confirmation |
| Account currency issues | Check the ad account currency and the card's supported currencies |
| Unpaid balance | Clear the outstanding balance in Billing & Payments before ads can run |

## Tips to Avoid Payment Problems

- **Add a backup payment method** so ads keep running if one card fails.
- **Set an account spending limit** to control budgets.
- **Check your bank's foreign-currency limit** before planning large campaigns.
- **Use Meta Business Suite** with a verified business, so ownership and billing are clear.
- **Never share your card details** with anyone who asks for them through Messenger or email. Fake "Meta support" messages are a common scam.

### Why does Meta keep declining my card?
Usually because international online payments are blocked, a foreign-currency limit has been reached, or billing details don't match your bank's records. Contact your bank first.

### Can I use a debit card for Facebook ads?
Sometimes. It must be enabled for international online transactions. Many businesses find credit cards more reliable.

### Is it safe to let an agency pay for my ads?
Yes, if the ad account is in your name, spend is transparent and fees are clearly separate.
