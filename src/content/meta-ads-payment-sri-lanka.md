# How to Pay for Facebook and Instagram Ads in Sri Lanka

Your ads are ready, your audience is set, and then Meta shows "payment failed". It's one of the most common problems Sri Lankan businesses face with Facebook and Instagram ads. This guide explains how Meta ads billing works, which payment options Sri Lankan advertisers can use, how local billing and agencies fit in, and how to fix declined payments.

Meta changes its billing options from time to time. Use [Meta's payment guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/212763688755026) and the options displayed in your own Billing & Payments settings; country and currency affect eligibility.

## How Meta Ads Billing Works

Facebook and Instagram ads are paid through **Meta Ads Manager**. Each ad account has:

- **A currency**, chosen when the account is created (it can't be changed later without a new ad account).
- **A billing country**, based on your business address.
- **A payment method**, such as a card or prepaid funds, where available.
- **A billing type:** either automatic payments (charged after you spend or reach a threshold) or manual/prepaid payments (you add funds first). Availability depends on your country and currency. Do not assume both types are offered to every Sri Lankan account; use the options displayed in your account.

## Payment Options for Sri Lankan Advertisers

| Option | How it works | Things to check |
|---|---|---|
| Credit card | Meta charges your card as you spend | Card must allow international online payments |
| Debit card | Same as credit card, if accepted | Many debit cards block foreign transactions by default |
| Prepaid / manual funds | Add funds before ads run | Only where available in your account; check country, currency and initial payment setup |
| Local billing / local currency | Pay in LKR through local methods, where offered | Do not assume LKR or local payment methods are offered; verify account options |
| Through an agency | The agency manages billing as part of its service | Check the account remains in your name and spend is transparent |

## What Is a Meta ASP or Authorised Reseller?

In some markets, Meta works with **authorised sales partners (ASPs)** or resellers who help businesses buy ads, sometimes with local invoicing. No Sri Lankan billing partner is verified in this guide. Check any claimed status in [Meta's partner directory](https://www.facebook.com/fbp/partner-directory), then confirm the exact billing service separately. Directory inclusion alone does not prove reseller status or local invoicing eligibility.

If you work with any reseller, make sure:
- You own the ad account, or you have full access to it.
- You can see the real ad spend in Ads Manager.
- Fees are clearly separated from ad spend.

## Why Your Card Gets Declined – and How to Fix It

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| International online payments are blocked | Ask your bank to enable online and foreign-currency transactions |
| Foreign-currency spending limit reached | Ask your bank about your annual or monthly limit |
| Card details or address don't match | Make sure billing name and address match the bank's records |
| Too many failed attempts | Wait, then retry once with the bank's confirmation |
| Account currency issues | Check the ad account currency and the card's supported currencies |
| Unpaid balance | Clear the outstanding balance in Billing & Payments before ads can run |

## Tips to Avoid Payment Problems

- **Add a backup payment method** so ads keep running if one card fails.
- **Set an account spending limit** to control budgets.
- **Check your bank's foreign-currency limit** before planning large campaigns.
- **Use Meta Business Suite** with a verified business, so ownership and billing are clear.
- **Never share your card details** with anyone who asks for them through Messenger or email. Fake "Meta support" messages are a common scam.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I pay for Facebook ads in LKR?
Do not assume LKR is selectable or that local payment methods are available. Check [Meta accepted currencies](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/151451521590943), its payment guidance and your account settings. A bank converting a foreign-currency charge to rupees is not the same as Meta billing in LKR.

### Why does Meta keep declining my card?
Usually because international online payments are blocked, a foreign-currency limit has been reached, or billing details don't match your bank's records. Contact your bank first.

### Can I use a debit card for Facebook ads?
Sometimes. It must be enabled for international online transactions. Many businesses find credit cards more reliable.

### Is it safe to let an agency pay for my ads?
Yes, if the ad account is in your name, spend is transparent and fees are clearly separate.
