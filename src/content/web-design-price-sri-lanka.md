## What Affects the Price of a Website?

- **Number of pages:** more pages means more design, content and testing.
- **Template vs custom design:** custom designs take longer but fit your brand and goals.
- **Content writing:** professionally written, SEO-friendly content adds cost but improves results.
- **E-commerce features:** product catalogues, payment gateways, delivery settings and stock management.
- **Integrations:** booking systems, CRM, WhatsApp, chat and Google Analytics.
- **Languages:** Sinhala, Tamil and English versions multiply content work.
- **SEO setup:** proper structure, speed, schema and tracking.
- **Revisions and support:** how many changes and how much after-launch help is included.

## Low-Cost Web Design – What's the Catch?

Low-cost websites can be a reasonable starting point, but check what's missing:

- **Template designs** shared with many other websites
- **Slow loading**, especially on mobile
- **No SEO setup**, so the site rarely appears on Google
- **No tracking**, so you can't tell where enquiries come from
- **You may not own the domain or hosting account**
- **Expensive changes later**, or no support at all

A cheap website that doesn't bring enquiries often ends up costing more than one built properly from the start.

## How to Get an Accurate Web Design Quote

1. **List the pages you need** (home, about, services, contact, blog).
2. **List must-have features** (WhatsApp button, booking, payments, languages).
3. **Share 2–3 websites you like** and explain why.
4. **Decide who writes the content.**
5. **Ask what's included after launch:** support, changes and training.
6. **Confirm ownership** of the domain, hosting and files.

## Is a Website Worth It if I'm Already on Facebook?

Yes. Social media brings attention, but your website builds trust, ranks on Google and makes your ads work harder. Many Sri Lankan customers check a business's website before calling, even after finding it on Facebook. If you plan to run ads, a fast landing page usually lowers your cost per lead. Usually yes, for the domain and hosting. Maintenance and updates may be optional.
