## About this Telesales Executive job in Sri Lanka

A Telesales Executive at Cypher Digital sells by phone, WhatsApp and video call. You speak with business owners and marketing managers who have already shown interest in growing online, understand what they need, recommend the right service or package, and guide them to sign up. It is a real sales role with real earning power: the more you close, the more you earn.

Sri Lankan businesses are moving online faster than ever. Hotels, restaurants, clothing brands, real estate companies, clinics, tuition classes and small shops all need more enquiries from Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google. Most of them do not know where to start. You are the friendly, knowledgeable voice that turns their interest into a clear plan, and that plan into a sale.

You do not need to be a marketing expert on day one. We teach you how each service works, what results clients can expect, and how to answer the most common questions. Training builds your product knowledge and communication skills.

### Who this job is for

- Ambitious school leavers (18+), undergraduates and graduates who want to earn based on effort, not just attendance.
- People with call center, retail, banking, insurance or field sales experience who want to move into the fast-growing digital industry.
- Confident communicators who enjoy talking to people and are motivated by targets and rewards.
- Parents, homemakers and people outside Colombo who want a serious income opportunity they can do from home.

## What you will sell: digital marketing services and products

You sell services that Sri Lankan businesses genuinely need and actively search for. Because our ad campaigns bring in enquiries every day, you spend your time talking to interested prospects instead of cold-calling random numbers.

Monthly retainers such as social media and ad management are especially valuable for you, with recurring commission eligibility defined in your written offer.

## How you earn: the commission model

Earnings are commission-based. Exact new-sale commission, recurring commission duration and rate, monthly targets, bonuses, upsell and referral rewards are confirmed in writing before you start. There is no confirmed base allowance or guaranteed monthly income stated here.

Commission is based on payments clients actually make. Your written offer must explain eligibility, cancellations and refunds, the payment date and method, and whether call costs are company-funded, reimbursed or paid by you. Do not assume a company SIM or a call allowance.

No registration fee, training fee, deposit or starter-kit fee is charged.

## Grow fast: your career path at Cypher Digital

Progression is based on results, client care, coaching ability and available positions. No fixed promotion deadline or leadership opening is guaranteed.

1. Telesales Executive Learn our services, follow the scripts, call warm leads and close your first sales. Focus: confidence, product knowledge and steady weekly numbers.
2. Senior Telesales Executive Progression depends on results, client care and an available role; no fixed promotion deadline is promised.
3. Team Lead Strong results plus the ability to coach. You train new executives, run daily huddles with any leadership compensation confirmed in writing.
4. Sales Manager or Key Account Manager Own a region, an industry such as hotels or real estate, or our biggest accounts, and help shape pricing and sales strategy. See our Sales Manager vacancy .

You can also move sideways. Many telesales people find they love the marketing side and move into Digital Marketing Executive , Business Development Executive or client account roles.

## Telesales Executive job description

### What you will do

- Call and message warm leads from Cypher Digital's own Facebook, Google and website campaigns, usually within hours of their enquiry.
- Understand each business: what they sell, who their customers are and what is holding back their growth.
- Recommend the right service or package and explain clearly what the client gets and when.
- Share proposals, quotes and case studies by WhatsApp and email, and follow up until a decision is made.
- Close sales and confirm the first payment following our process.
- Record every call, outcome and next step in our CRM or shared sheet.
- Hand new clients to the account team smoothly and spot upsell opportunities.
- Meet your weekly and monthly call, meeting and sales targets.

### What we are looking for

- Age 18 or older; A/L qualified or above preferred.
- Clear, confident speaking in Sinhala, Tamil or English. Two or more languages is a big advantage.
- A positive attitude, resilience and the drive to hit targets. Hearing "no" does not stop you.
- Basic computer and smartphone skills: WhatsApp Business, Google Sheets, email.
- For remote work: a reliable phone, stable internet and a quiet place to make calls.
- Sales, telemarketing, call center or customer service experience is welcome but not required.

### What you get

- Commission on every sale with no earnings cap, plus target bonuses and recurring commission on monthly clients.
- Warm leads from our in-house marketing team, so you are not cold-calling all day.
- Structured training, sales scripts, objection-handling guides and real case studies.
- A sales coach and team WhatsApp group for daily support.
- Clear, results-based promotion to Senior Executive, Team Lead and Manager.
- Office or work-from-home, full-time or part-time.
- Experience selling digital marketing services to Sri Lankan businesses.

### A typical day

Actual full-time hours and part-time calling blocks are agreed before starting. A day may include a team check-in, fresh-lead calls, proposal follow-ups, a client video call and CRM updates.

Part-time executives choose agreed calling blocks, such as mornings or evenings, within business hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does a Telesales Executive do?

You call businesses interested in digital marketing, understand their needs, explain services, follow up and close sales. This differs from the project-based lead-follow-up role, which records enquiries for another sales team.

### How much can I earn and is there a basic allowance?

Earnings are commission-based. No numeric rate, base allowance or guaranteed monthly income is confirmed here. Request the new-sale and recurring rates, targets and bonus conditions in your written offer.

### Can I work from home or part-time?

Yes. Full-time and part-time options are available from the Colombo office or fully from home anywhere in Sri Lanka. Remote applicants need a reliable phone, internet and a quiet workspace. Calling hours are agreed with the team.

### Do I need experience or pay anything to join?

Experience is welcome but not required. Training and scripts are supplied. Applicants must be 18 or older. There is no registration fee, training fee or deposit.

### Who pays for calls and when is commission paid?

Agree call-cost coverage, any limit, payment dates and payment method in writing before starting. Commission eligibility is based on collected client payments; refund and cancellation adjustments must also be explained in your offer.
