## Ad problems we fix

### Disabled or restricted ad accounts
Meta disables ad accounts when its systems think something breaks the rules. Often it's a misunderstanding: a new account that spent too fast, a payment that failed, a page name that looks suspicious, or an old ad that broke a policy you didn't know about. We review what triggered the restriction, prepare a clear appeal, and fix the cause so it doesn't happen again.

### Payment declined and billing problems
Card payment issues are one of the most common problems for Sri Lankan advertisers. Your bank may block international online payments, your card may have a foreign currency limit, or Meta may flag a mismatch between your card, your business details and your ad account currency. We check your billing setup, help you choose the right payment method and currency, and set up the account so payments go through.

### Ads rejected or "in review" for days
An ad can be rejected for the wording, the image, the landing page, or the category your product falls into (health, finance, property, education and employment ads have extra rules). We read the rejection reason, find exactly what triggered it, and rewrite or redesign the ad so it's approved, without losing the message you want to send.

### Lost access to your Facebook page or Business Manager
A former employee, a freelancer or an old agency still owns your page or ad account. Or you lost access to the only admin profile. We help you recover access through Meta's official processes and set up Business Portfolio (Business Manager) properly, with you as the owner.

### Ads running but no results
Your ads are spending but nobody is messaging, buying or calling. Usually the cause is the wrong campaign objective, an audience that's too broad or too small, a broken pixel, or a landing page that doesn't load on mobile. We audit the campaign and tell you what to change.

### Pixel, Conversions API and tracking
If your Meta pixel isn't tracking purchases or leads, Meta can't optimise your ads. We install and test the pixel and events on your website, so you can see which ads actually bring sales.

LinkedIn ads cost more per click, so mistakes cost more too. We set up Campaign Manager, connect your company page, fix billing and targeting, and make sure your B2B ads reach decision-makers. See our [LinkedIn marketing services](/linkedin-marketing-sri-lanka).

## Why Sri Lankan businesses get stuck with ad problems

Ad platforms are built for global advertisers. Sri Lankan businesses run into a few problems more often than others:

- **International payments.** Ads are usually billed in US dollars or with international card processing. Some local cards are not enabled for online foreign payments, or have low limits. A single failed payment can pause all your ads and sometimes restricts the account.
- **Business verification documents.** Meta and Google sometimes ask you to verify your business. Your business registration, utility bill and website must show the same business name and address. Small differences in spelling between Sinhala, Tamil and English documents can cause rejections.
- **Special ad categories.** Ads for property, jobs, loans, education courses and health services have stricter targeting rules. Many local businesses don't know they're in a special category until their ads are rejected.
- **Shared and old accounts.** Pages are often created by a staff member, a friend or a previous agency on a personal profile. When that person leaves, the business loses control.
- **Boosting from the page.** The "Boost post" button is easy, but it gives you fewer controls and it's harder to fix when something goes wrong. If you mostly boost posts, see our [social media boosting service](/social-media-boosting-service-sri-lanka).

None of these problems mean your business did anything wrong. They mean your account needs to be set up the way the platforms expect.
