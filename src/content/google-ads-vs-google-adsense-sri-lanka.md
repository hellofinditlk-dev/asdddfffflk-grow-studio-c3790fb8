# Google Ads vs Google AdSense in Sri Lanka – What's the Difference?

Google Ads and Google AdSense have similar names, both come from Google, and both involve ads. But they do opposite jobs. **Google Ads is for businesses that want to advertise. Google AdSense is for website owners who want to earn money by showing ads.**

If you're a Sri Lankan business owner trying to get more customers, you need Google Ads – and often a [digital marketing agency in Sri Lanka](/) to run it well. If you run a blog, news site or YouTube channel and want to earn from your traffic, you're looking for AdSense. This guide explains both, how they connect, and which one is right for you.

## What Is Google AdSense?

Google AdSense is a programme that lets website owners place ads on their sites and earn money when visitors see or click those ads. Google finds the advertisers, chooses relevant ads and pays the website owner a share of the revenue.

In Sri Lanka, AdSense is used by:

- News and entertainment websites
- Bloggers writing about travel, food, technology or education
- Recipe, lyrics and tutorial websites
- Websites with large amounts of regular traffic

How much you earn depends on your traffic, your audience's location and the topics you write about. Sites with more visitors from high-value advertising markets usually earn more per visitor.

## What Is Google Ads (Formerly AdWords)?

Google Ads is Google's advertising platform. Businesses use it to show ads when people search on Google, watch YouTube, use Google Maps or browse websites. It was called **Google AdWords** until 2018, which is why many people in Sri Lanka still search for "AdWords".

With Google Ads you can:

- Appear at the top of Google when people search for your products or services
- Show video ads on YouTube
- Show banner ads on websites, including websites that use AdSense
- Remarket to people who already visited your website
- Set your own budget and only pay for clicks or views

## How Google Ads and AdSense Connect

The two platforms are two sides of the same system:

1. A **business** creates a Display campaign in Google Ads and pays to show banner ads.
2. A **website owner** uses AdSense and makes space on their website for ads.
3. Google matches the two, showing the business's ad on the publisher's website.
4. The business pays Google; Google pays part of that to the website owner.

So when your Google Ads Display campaign appears on a Sri Lankan news website, that website is probably earning through AdSense. To understand when Display ads make sense compared with Search ads, read our guide to [Search vs Display ads](/blog/google-search-ads-vs-display-ads-difference).

## Which One Does Your Business Need?

Not sure where to advertise at all? Google Ads captures people already searching, while [social media advertising in Sri Lanka](/social-media-advertising-sri-lanka) reaches people before they search. Many businesses use both.

**Choose Google Ads if:**
- You sell products or services and want more customers
- You want enquiries, calls, WhatsApp messages or online sales
- You want to appear at the top of Google for searches related to your business

**Choose AdSense if:**
- You run a content website or blog with regular traffic
- You want to earn money from visitors, not sell your own products
- You're comfortable with other brands' ads appearing on your site

**Can a business use both?** Yes, but it's rarely a good idea on a business website. AdSense ads can show competitors' offers and send your visitors away. Most businesses should use Google Ads to bring customers in, and keep their own website focused on converting them.
