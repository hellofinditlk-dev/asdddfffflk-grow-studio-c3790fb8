# YouTube SEO in Sri Lanka – How to Get Your Videos Found

YouTube is one of the most-used platforms in Sri Lanka, and it's also a search engine. People search it for product reviews, tutorials, recipes, property tours, courses and much more. YouTube SEO is the process of optimising your videos and channel so they appear when Sri Lankans search, and keep getting recommended long after you publish.

This guide covers practical YouTube SEO for Sri Lankan businesses, including how to handle Sinhala, Tamil and English searches.

## How YouTube Decides Which Videos to Show

YouTube's search and recommendation systems look at:

- **Relevance:** does the title, description and content match what the viewer searched?
- **Engagement:** do people click, keep watching, like, comment and subscribe?
- **Satisfaction:** do viewers watch until the end, or leave quickly?
- **Viewer history:** what each person has watched before.

In short: optimise for the search, then make a video people actually want to watch.

## Step 1: Keyword Research for Sri Lankan Audiences

- **Use YouTube search suggestions:** type your topic and note the autocomplete phrases.
- **Search in the language your audience uses:** Sinhala script, Singlish (Sinhala in English letters), Tamil or English. Many Sri Lankans search in Singlish.
- **Check competing videos:** look at titles and view counts for the top results.
- **Use Google Trends** with YouTube Search selected and Sri Lanka as the location.
- **Look at your Google Search Console data:** questions people ask Google often work as video topics.

## Step 2: Write a Title That Ranks and Gets Clicked

- Put the main keyword near the start.
- Keep it clear and specific (around 60 characters is a good guide).
- Add the location or language if it matters, e.g. "in Sri Lanka" or "Sinhala".
- Promise a clear result: "How to…", "Price list 2026", "Full tour".

## Step 3: Thumbnails

Thumbnails drive clicks, and clicks drive rankings.

- Use a clear face or product close-up.
- Use 2–4 large words at most.
- Use high contrast so it stands out on a phone.
- Keep the style consistent across your channel.

## Step 4: Description, Chapters and Tags

- **First two lines:** summarise the video with the main keyword. They show in search results.
- **Chapters:** add timestamps so viewers and Google can jump to sections.
- **Links:** add your website, WhatsApp and related videos.
- **Tags:** less important than they used to be, but add your main keyword and common spelling variations.

## Step 5: Captions and Language

- Upload accurate captions rather than relying only on auto-captions, especially for Sinhala and Tamil.
- Consider separate videos for Sinhala and English audiences instead of mixing languages.
- Add translated titles and descriptions where YouTube allows it.

## Step 6: Watch Time and Retention

- **Hook in the first 10–15 seconds.** Tell viewers what they'll get.
- **Cut the long intros.**
- **Show, don't just talk:** demos, screens, before-and-after.
- **End with a next video**, using end screens and playlists to keep viewers watching.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does YouTube SEO take to work?
Some videos get search views within days, but most channels need consistent uploads over several months to grow steadily.

### Should I make videos in Sinhala or English?
Choose the language your customers search in. Many Sri Lankan businesses do best with Sinhala or Singlish titles for local audiences, and English for professional or overseas audiences.

### Do tags still matter on YouTube?
They play a small role. Titles, thumbnails, descriptions and watch time matter much more.
