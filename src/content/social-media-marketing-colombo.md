## How social media works differently in Colombo

Colombo audiences are not the same as audiences elsewhere in Sri Lanka. We plan around that:

**Language mix.** Colombo audiences switch between English, Sinhala and Tamil. Many Colombo brands do best with English visuals and Sinhala or Tamil captions, or separate versions of key ads. We test what works for your audience instead of guessing.

**Commute and lunch-hour timing.** A lot of Colombo scrolling happens on the morning and evening commute and at lunch. We schedule posts and ad delivery around when your customers are actually on their phones.

**Area-level targeting.** A café in Colombo 7 and a hardware store in Maharagama need very different audiences. We target by area and radius, so you're not paying to reach people who will never visit.

**High competition.** Colombo feeds are crowded with brands. Your content needs to stop the scroll in the first second, with clear offers and strong short video.

**WhatsApp-first buying.** Most Colombo customers want to message before they buy. We set up ads and posts so the next step is a WhatsApp message to you.

## Content that works for Colombo businesses

Colombo customers see hundreds of brand posts a day. These are the types of content that consistently stand out:

**Behind-the-scenes reels.** Your kitchen at lunch rush, your stylist at work, your team packing orders. Real people and real places build trust faster than polished graphics.

**Clear offers with prices.** "Lunch rice and curry, LKR 950, delivered in Colombo 3, 4 and 5" gets more messages than "Visit us today!" Colombo customers compare quickly, so show them the price.

**Customer stories.** Short clips or quotes from happy customers, with their permission. One real review in a reel often beats a week of product posts.

**Location-based content.** Posts that mention a landmark, an area or a local event ("two minutes from Liberty Plaza", "free parking in Rajagiriya") help people picture visiting you.

**How-to and tips.** A salon showing a five-minute hairstyle, a hardware shop explaining which paint to use in the monsoon, a clinic answering common questions. Helpful content gets saved and shared.

**Seasonal moments.** Colombo responds strongly to the calendar. Plan around it rather than posting a greeting on the day.
