## Choose the Right Objective

Instagram ads are run through Meta Ads Manager, and the objective tells Meta what result to find for you:

- **Awareness:** reach as many people in your audience as possible.
- **Traffic:** send people to your website or landing page.
- **Engagement and messages:** start conversations in Instagram DMs or WhatsApp.
- **Leads:** collect enquiries using instant forms, without leaving Instagram.
- **Sales:** drive website purchases, tracked with the Meta Pixel.

Choosing the right objective is one of the biggest factors in cost and results. Many businesses in Sri Lanka get the best results from message campaigns, because customers prefer to ask questions in DMs or on WhatsApp before buying.

## Targeting for a Small Market

Sri Lanka is a smaller market than many countries, so targeting needs care. Too narrow and ads become expensive; too broad and they reach the wrong people.

- **Location:** island-wide, Colombo and suburbs, or a radius around your store.
- **Age and gender:** based on who actually buys from you.
- **Interests and behaviours:** fashion, travel, food, fitness, technology and more.
- **Lookalike audiences:** people similar to your customers or followers.
- **Retargeting:** people who visited your website, watched your Reels or engaged with your profile.

## Creative That Works on Instagram

Instagram ads succeed when they look like content, not adverts:

- **Vertical video first.** Shoot and edit in 9:16 for Reels and Stories.
- **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** Show the product, the result or the person straight away.
- **Captions on screen.** Many people watch without sound.
- **Real people and real products.** Behind-the-scenes, try-ons, before-and-after and customer reviews.
- **Sinhala, English or both.** Match the language your customers use.
- **One clear action.** Shop now, send a message, book now.

## Boost Button vs Ads Manager – Why It Matters

The "Boost" button inside Instagram is quick, but limited. It offers fewer objectives, basic targeting and almost no control over placements or testing.

We run every campaign in **Meta Ads Manager**, which allows:

- The right objective for your goal
- Detailed audiences and exclusions
- A/B testing of creative and audiences
- Conversion tracking with the Meta Pixel
- Combined campaigns across Instagram and Facebook

If you're wondering how to run Meta ads in Sri Lanka properly, this is the difference. If you've only boosted posts so far, our guide on [how to run Instagram ads](/blog/how-to-run-instagram-ads-sri-lanka) explains it step by step.
