## YouTube Ad Formats

| Format | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Skippable in-stream | Plays before or during videos; viewers can skip after 5 seconds | Storytelling, offers, lead generation |
| Non-skippable in-stream | Short ad that must be watched in full | Key messages that must be seen |
| Bumper ads | Up to 6 seconds, non-skippable | Brand recall, product launches, reminders |
| In-feed video ads | Appear in YouTube search results and recommendations | Tutorials, reviews, longer content |
| YouTube Shorts ads | Vertical ads between Shorts | Reaching younger, mobile-first viewers |

Most campaigns use a mix: a longer skippable ad to explain, and bumper ads to repeat the message.

## Targeting Options

- **Demographics:** age, gender, parental status, household income (where available).
- **Location:** all of Sri Lanka, specific districts or cities, or a radius around your business.
- **Interests and habits:** people interested in travel, cars, education, home improvement and more.
- **Custom segments:** people who searched for specific keywords on Google or visited similar websites.
- **Life events:** people moving home, getting married or starting a business.
- **Remarketing:** people who visited your website or watched your videos. See [Google remarketing](/google-remarketing-sri-lanka).
- **Placements:** specific YouTube channels or videos your audience watches.

## YouTube Ads Cost in Sri Lanka

YouTube ads are usually charged per view (CPV) or per 1,000 impressions (CPM). A view is typically counted when someone watches 30 seconds, the whole ad if it's shorter, or interacts with it.

- **Cost per view:** no transparent Sri Lanka-wide CPV benchmark was verified. Use a Sri Lanka-targeted Reach Planner forecast where available and report actual campaign CPV.
- **Cost per 1,000 impressions:** varies by audience and video format; use the account forecast rather than treating an overseas CPM as a local price.
- **Planning example, not a local benchmark:** monthly spend = target views × forecast CPV, or target impressions ÷ 1,000 × forecast CPM. Creative and management costs are separate.

You set the budget and can pause at any time. For overall Google Ads pricing, see [Google Ads Sri Lanka](/google-ads-sri-lanka).

## Creative That Works on YouTube

The video makes or breaks a YouTube campaign. Our guidelines:

- **Hook in the first 5 seconds.** Show the brand and the reason to watch before the skip button appears.
- **Design for sound off.** Add captions and on-screen text, as many people watch on mute.
- **One clear message.** One product, one offer, one action.
- **Make language versions.** Sinhala and English versions usually outperform one mixed version.
- **Clear call to action.** Tell viewers exactly what to do next: visit, call, WhatsApp or book.
- **Vertical edits for Shorts.** Re-cut horizontal videos into 9:16 for Shorts placements.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum budget for YouTube ads in Sri Lanka?
There is no single Sri Lankan monthly minimum suitable for every YouTube campaign. Choose the video objective first, then use the audience forecast and a spend limit you can afford to test. Ask for separate estimates for media, video production and management.

### Do I need a YouTube channel?
Yes. YouTube ads run from a video uploaded to a YouTube channel. The video can be unlisted, and we can help set the channel up.

### Can you make the video for us?
Yes. Our team handles scripting, filming, editing and AI video, and delivers versions sized for YouTube and Shorts.

### Skippable or bumper ads – which is better?
They do different jobs. Skippable ads explain and persuade; bumper ads repeat a short message cheaply. Most campaigns use both.

### Can YouTube ads generate leads?
Yes. With a clear call to action, a good landing page and conversion tracking, YouTube can drive enquiries, especially when combined with remarketing.
