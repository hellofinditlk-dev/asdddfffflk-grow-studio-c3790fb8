## What Are Google Shopping Ads and Performance Max?

**Google Shopping ads** are product listings that appear on Google Search, the Shopping tab, Google Images and partner sites. Instead of text, shoppers see a photo, product name, price and your store name before they click, so the people who click are usually ready to buy.

**Performance Max** is a Google Ads campaign type that uses your product feed, images, videos and text to show ads across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Maps from one campaign. Google's automation decides where to show ads, based on the conversion goals you set.

Both are powered by your product feed, the list of products, prices and details you send to Google Merchant Center.

## Merchant Center Setup and Product Feed Optimisation

Your product feed decides which searches your products appear for and how attractive they look. We handle:

- **Merchant Center setup:** account creation, website verification, business information, shipping and return policies.
- **Feed connection:** linking your Shopify, WooCommerce or custom store so products and prices stay up to date.

## Shopping vs Performance Max vs Search – When to Use Each

| Campaign type | Strength | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Shopping | Full control over products and bids | Stores that want detailed control and clear reporting |
| Performance Max | Reaches every Google channel from one campaign | Stores with good conversion data and strong creative |
| Search | Text ads for specific keywords | Brand searches, high-intent product keywords, services |

Many stores use a combination: Performance Max for reach, Standard Shopping for key products, and Search for brand terms.

## Bidding for ROAS and Profit

Clicks are not the goal. Sales are. We manage Shopping campaigns around **return on ad spend (ROAS)**: how much revenue you earn for every rupee spent on ads.

- Grouping products by margin, price and performance
- Setting realistic ROAS targets based on your profit margins
- Pushing budget to best-sellers and cutting products that don't convert
- Excluding products that are out of stock or unprofitable to advertise
- Combining Shopping with [Google remarketing](/google-remarketing-sri-lanka) to win back shoppers who didn't buy
