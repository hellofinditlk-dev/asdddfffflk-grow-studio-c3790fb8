## What a Google Ads Specialist Does

A Google Ads specialist – sometimes called a PPC or search engine marketing specialist – is responsible for turning your budget into results. Day to day, that means:

- **Keyword research:** finding the searches your customers actually use, in English and Sinhala/Singlish, and the ones to avoid.
- **Campaign structure:** organising campaigns and ad groups so each ad matches what people search for.
- **Ad copy and assets:** writing headlines, descriptions, sitelinks, call and WhatsApp extensions that earn clicks.
- **Conversion tracking:** setting up tracking for calls, forms, WhatsApp clicks and purchases, so success is measured in leads, not clicks.
- **Bidding and budgets:** choosing the right bidding strategy and moving budget to what works.
- **Ongoing optimisation:** reviewing search terms, adding negative keywords, testing ads and improving Quality Score.
- **Reporting:** explaining what happened, what it cost and what we'll do next, in plain language.

## Why Hire a Specialist Instead of Running Ads Yourself

Google Ads looks simple: set a budget, choose keywords, write an ad. But the default settings are built to spend money, not to save it. Common problems we see when business owners run ads themselves include:

- Broad keywords triggering ads for unrelated searches
- No conversion tracking, so nobody knows which keywords bring customers
- Ads shown across the whole island when the business only serves one city
- Display network switched on by default, spending budget on low-quality placements
- No time to check the account each week

A specialist removes that waste and spends their time improving your results, while you focus on running your business.
