## What drives the cost of social media marketing in Sri Lanka?

Five things decide most of your monthly cost.

### 1. How much content you need
More posts take more planning, writing and design time. Twelve good posts a month is enough for most small businesses. Brands with daily offers, new stock every week, or several product lines need more.

### 2. Video and shoots
Video costs more than images because it needs filming, editing, music and captions. Reels made from stock footage cost less. Reels shot at your actual location look more authentic and usually perform better, but need a crew's time. Shoots outside Colombo may add travel costs.

### 3. Number of platforms
Each platform has its own formats and audience. Cross-posting the same content to TikTok costs little extra. A real TikTok strategy, with content made for TikTok trends, takes more work. LinkedIn content for business audiences is written differently from Facebook content.

### 4. Paid advertising
Ads have two costs: the budget you pay Meta to show your ads, and the fee for someone to plan, set up, test and optimise the campaigns. You can boost posts yourself, but campaigns run by a specialist usually bring more messages for the same budget.

### 5. Languages
Creating content in English, Sinhala and Tamil takes more writing and design time than a single language, but often reaches far more customers.

## Agency fee vs ad budget: what's the difference?

This is the part most businesses find confusing, so we keep it simple.

**Agency fee: what you pay Cypher Digital.** This covers planning, design, video, writing, publishing, page handling, ads management and reporting.

**Ad budget: what you pay Meta (Facebook and Instagram).** This is the money Meta charges to show your ads to people. You pay it directly from your own ad account with your own card. We never hold your ad money.

Why keep them separate? So you always know exactly how much goes to ads and how much goes to the work. If someone quotes one "all-in" price for social media and ads, ask how much of it is actually going to Meta.

## How to tell if your social media spend is working

Before you decide whether a cost is "too much", decide how you'll measure what it brings in. Watch these numbers each month:

- **Messages and inquiries:** how many WhatsApp messages, calls or forms came from social media.
- **Cost per inquiry:** your total monthly spend divided by the number of inquiries.
- **Inquiries that became customers:** only you know this, so keep a simple count.
- **Value of a customer:** what an average customer spends with you, including repeat visits.

If one new customer is worth LKR 50,000 to your business, a few new customers a month cover the cost of a package and ads. If each customer is worth LKR 1,000, you'll need far more volume, and a smaller plan or boosting-only may make more sense. We'll work through this with you on the strategy call.

## How to get more for your budget

1. **Start with fewer platforms, done well.** Facebook and Instagram first. Add more once they're working.
2. **Put money behind your best posts only.** Promote posts with a clear offer, not greetings. See our [social media boosting service](/social-media-boosting-service-sri-lanka).
3. **Batch your shoots.** One four-hour shoot can produce a month of reels and photos.
4. **Give ads time.** The first two to three weeks are a learning period. Stopping and starting wastes budget.
5. **Reply fast.** Ads that bring WhatsApp messages only work if someone answers quickly.
6. **Track results.** Know your cost per message or lead, not just likes and reach.
