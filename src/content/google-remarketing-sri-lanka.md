## What Is Google Remarketing?

Google remarketing (also called retargeting) shows ads to people who have already interacted with your business, such as visiting your website, watching your videos or sharing their details with you.

These people already know you, so they are far more likely to enquire than someone seeing your brand for the first time. Remarketing usually costs less per lead than reaching new audiences, which makes it one of the most efficient parts of any Google Ads account.

## Types of Google Remarketing

| Type | Where ads show | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Standard display remarketing | Websites and apps in Google's Display Network | Reminding visitors about your brand or offer |
| Dynamic remarketing | Display, with the exact products people viewed | E-commerce stores |
| Search remarketing (RLSA) | Google Search, when past visitors search again | Bidding higher for warm visitors |
| YouTube remarketing | YouTube, for site visitors or video viewers | Video reminders and offers |
| Customer Match | Search, YouTube, Gmail, Display | Reaching existing customers and leads using your own lists (subject to Google's eligibility requirements) |

## Audience Lists We Build

## Creative and Frequency – Not Annoying Your Audience

Remarketing done badly follows people around with the same ad for weeks. We avoid that by:

- **Frequency caps:** limiting how many times one person sees your ads each day or week.
- **Membership durations:** removing people from lists after a sensible time.
- **Fresh creative:** rotating ads so people see new reasons to come back.
- **Sequenced messages:** first a reminder, then a testimonial, then an offer.
- **Excluding converters:** stopping ads once someone has enquired or bought.

Remarketing is not just for e-commerce. It works well for:

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many website visitors do I need for remarketing?
Google's current guidance lists at least **100 active visitors or users in the last 30 days** for website data segments on Display, Search and YouTube. Eligible active users are not the same as total website visits, and network compatibility and policy checks still apply. Older Customer Match lists uploaded before 1 February 2024 retain a different 1,000-matched-user requirement unless refreshed. Check Audience manager for the segment's actual serving status. [Source: Google data-segment requirements](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2472738?hl=en).

### How long do people stay on remarketing lists?
You choose. We usually set durations based on how long customers take to decide, from a few days for e-commerce to several months for property or education.

### Does remarketing work for services, not just online shops?
Yes. Service businesses often see some of their best cost per lead from remarketing, because visitors are already researching the service.

### Will remarketing annoy people?
Not when it's managed properly. We use frequency caps, rotate creative and stop showing ads to people who have already converted.

### Do I need tracking set up first?
Yes. A Google tag needs to be installed on your website to build audiences. We handle the setup and check it's working correctly.
