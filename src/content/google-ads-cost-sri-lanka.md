## How Much Do Google Ads Cost in Sri Lanka?

There is no single price, because Google Ads works like an auction. Every time someone searches, Google decides which ads to show and how much each click costs, based on how many businesses are bidding for that keyword and how relevant each ad is.

In practice, your total cost has three parts:

- **Cost per click (CPC):** what you pay each time someone clicks your ad. There is no independently verified Sri Lanka-wide CPC range in the sources reviewed. Estimate your keywords with Sri Lanka selected in Google's Keyword Planner, then compare the forecast with actual campaign CPC.
- **Daily or monthly budget:** the maximum you allow Google to spend. You control this completely and can change it at any time.
- **Management fee:** what you pay an agency or specialist to plan, build, monitor and improve your campaigns.

A small local business can test Google Ads with a modest budget. A competitive business, such as a private hospital, international school or real estate developer, usually needs a larger budget to win enough clicks to generate a steady flow of leads.

## What Drives Your Google Ads Cost

Two businesses can bid on the same keyword and pay very different prices. These are the main factors:

### Keyword competition
Keywords that many businesses want, such as "real estate Colombo" or "private hospital Sri Lanka", cost more per click than specific long-tail keywords.

### Quality Score
Google rates your ads from 1 to 10 based on expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience. A higher Quality Score lowers your cost per click, so a well-built account can pay less than a competitor for the same position.

### Match types and negative keywords
Broad keywords without negative keywords waste money on irrelevant searches. Careful match types and a strong negative keyword list are one of the fastest ways to cut cost.

### Location and schedule
Targeting all of Sri Lanka costs more than targeting the areas you actually serve. Showing ads only during business hours, when your team can answer calls, also reduces waste.

### Landing page
If people click but don't enquire, every click becomes more expensive per lead. A fast, clear, mobile-friendly page with WhatsApp and call buttons improves results without increasing your budget. ## Search vs Display vs YouTube vs Shopping – Cost Differences

| Campaign type | How you pay | Typical cost level | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search ads | Per click | Highest per click, highest intent | People actively looking for your service |
| Display ads | Per click or per 1,000 impressions | Low per click | Awareness and remarketing |
| YouTube ads | Per view or per 1,000 impressions | Low per view | Brand awareness and video storytelling |

## Google Ads Cost vs Facebook Ads Cost

Google Ads and Facebook Ads are priced differently because people use them differently.

- **Google Ads** reaches people who are already searching for what you sell. Clicks usually cost more, but intent is high, so leads are often ready to buy.
- **Facebook and Instagram ads** reach people based on interests and behaviour while they scroll. Clicks are usually cheaper, but people may need more convincing.

Many Sri Lankan businesses use both: Google to capture existing demand, and social ads to create new demand and retarget visitors. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Google Ads expensive in Sri Lanka?
Not necessarily. You choose your own budget and can start small. Google Ads only becomes expensive when campaigns are poorly targeted and pay for clicks that never turn into enquiries. With good keyword selection and tracking, many businesses get leads at a lower cost than traditional advertising.

### What is the minimum daily budget for Google Ads?
There is no universal Sri Lankan daily budget we can responsibly quote. Use your Sri Lanka keyword forecast and affordable monthly spend to choose it. For most campaigns, Google's monthly spending limit is based on **30.4 × the average daily budget**; some days can spend up to twice the average daily budget. For example, LKR 1,000 per day corresponds to a LKR 30,400 monthly limit under that rule, not a recommendation or guaranteed spend. [Source: Google budget spending limits](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637?hl=en).

### Do I pay Google directly?
Yes. Your ad spend is billed by Google directly to your payment method. Our management fee is invoiced separately, so you always know what goes to Google and what goes to us.

### How is the management fee charged?
Request a written quote for your account. It should state whether management is a fixed fee, a percentage of media spend or another agreed model, and whether setup, creative, landing pages, taxes and reporting are included. No unconfirmed starting price or billing model is advertised here.

### How soon will I see results?
Ads can start showing within a day of approval, and many businesses receive enquiries in the first week. Cost per lead usually improves over the first 60–90 days as we refine keywords and bidding.

### Can I pay for Google Ads in LKR?
Billing currency and payment options depend on the account's billing country, currency and payment setting. Confirm the options in your Google Ads Billing settings before funding the account; if it is billed in another currency, bank conversion charges and applicable taxes are separate from ad spend. We have not verified a universal LKR billing option for every Sri Lankan account. [Source: Google Ads payment methods](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375433?hl=en-GB).

### Is Google AdWords the same as Google Ads?
Yes. Google AdWords was renamed Google Ads in 2018. If you've seen "Google AdWords Sri Lanka" pricing, it refers to the same platform and the same costs explained on this page.
