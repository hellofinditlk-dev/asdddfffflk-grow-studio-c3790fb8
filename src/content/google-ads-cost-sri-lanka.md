## How Much Do Google Ads Cost in Sri Lanka?

There is no single price, because Google Ads works like an auction. Every time someone searches, Google decides which ads to show and how much each click costs, based on how many businesses are bidding for that keyword and how relevant each ad is.

In practice, your total cost has three parts:

- **Cost per click (CPC):** what you pay each time someone clicks your ad. In Sri Lanka this ranges from [ADD REAL CPC RANGE] depending on the industry and keyword.
- **Daily or monthly budget:** the maximum you allow Google to spend. You control this completely and can change it at any time.
- **Management fee:** what you pay an agency or specialist to plan, build, monitor and improve your campaigns.

A small local business can test Google Ads with a modest budget. A competitive business, such as a private hospital, international school or real estate developer, usually needs a larger budget to win enough clicks to generate a steady flow of leads.

## What Drives Your Google Ads Cost

Two businesses can bid on the same keyword and pay very different prices. These are the main factors:

### Keyword competition
Keywords that many businesses want, such as "real estate Colombo" or "private hospital Sri Lanka", cost more per click than specific long-tail keywords.

### Quality Score
Google rates your ads from 1 to 10 based on expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience. A higher Quality Score lowers your cost per click, so a well-built account can pay less than a competitor for the same position.

### Match types and negative keywords
Broad keywords without negative keywords waste money on irrelevant searches. Careful match types and a strong negative keyword list are one of the fastest ways to cut cost.

### Location and schedule
Targeting all of Sri Lanka costs more than targeting the areas you actually serve. Showing ads only during business hours, when your team can answer calls, also reduces waste.

### Landing page
If people click but don't enquire, every click becomes more expensive per lead. A fast, clear, mobile-friendly page with WhatsApp and call buttons improves results without increasing your budget. ## Google Ads Cost vs Facebook Ads Cost

Google Ads and Facebook Ads are priced differently because people use them differently.

- **Google Ads** reaches people who are already searching for what you sell. Clicks usually cost more, but intent is high, so leads are often ready to buy.
- **Facebook and Instagram ads** reach people based on interests and behaviour while they scroll. Clicks are usually cheaper, but people may need more convincing.

Many Sri Lankan businesses use both: Google to capture existing demand, and social ads to create new demand and retarget visitors.
