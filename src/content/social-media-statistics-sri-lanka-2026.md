# Social Media Statistics Sri Lanka 2026 – Usage, Platforms and Trends

How many people in Sri Lanka use social media, which platforms are growing, and when are people online? This page brings together the latest social media statistics for Sri Lanka and explains what they mean for businesses planning their marketing in 2026.

**Data reference:** DataReportal's Digital 2026 report mainly describes October/late 2025, not a full-year 2026 census. Figures below were checked against the [original Sri Lanka report](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sri-lanka) on 25 October 2026. User identities, ad reach, registered members and mobile connections are different measures.

TikTok is adult-only in this table; LinkedIn counts members. Do not add the rows together, infer nationwide market share, or use reported TikTok reach as proof that every Sri Lankan advertiser can open an eligible ad account.

### What this means for businesses
Facebook and YouTube have substantial reported advertising audiences. TikTok also has a large reported adult audience; Instagram and LinkedIn provide different campaign contexts. Select channels using your actual customer profile and account eligibility, not a claim that these totals prove age preferences or sales performance.

## Social Media Users by Age and Gender

A comparable nationwide distribution across 18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54 and 55+ was not verified in the report text. We do not fill those bands with guessed percentages. Use dated, platform-specific planning tools or your own audience insights; disclose the platform and denominator.

The report's combined social media identity estimate is 43.4% female and 56.6% male in late 2025. These source categories are not a census of individuals. Platform-level gender shares have different denominators and can fail to sum to 100%; do not mix them with this overall measure. Source: [DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sri-lanka).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many people use social media in Sri Lanka?
DataReportal reports 9.00 million social media user identities in October 2025, equivalent to 38.7% of the population. Identities are not necessarily unique people. Source: [Digital 2026: Sri Lanka](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sri-lanka).

### What is the most popular social media platform in Sri Lanka?
Facebook has the largest reported potential advertising audience among the listed comparable Facebook, YouTube and Instagram figures: 9.00 million versus 8.82 million and 2.25 million in late 2025. This is not a ranking of unique active users across all services.

### Is TikTok popular in Sri Lanka?
DataReportal reports a TikTok potential ad audience of 6.79 million adults in late 2025 and a reported increase of 25.5% from end 2024. This is an adult advertising estimate, not a unique-user census or proof of advertiser account eligibility.

### Which platform is best for business in Sri Lanka?
It depends on your audience. Facebook suits broad audiences, Instagram and TikTok suit younger audiences, YouTube suits video storytelling and LinkedIn suits B2B. Most businesses use two or three platforms together.
