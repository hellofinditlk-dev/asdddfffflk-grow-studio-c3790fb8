## When Are Sri Lankans Most Active Online?

Activity usually peaks in the evening after work and school, with smaller peaks at lunchtime and early morning commutes. Exact peak times vary by platform, audience and industry, so always check your own page insights.

## What These Statistics Mean for Your Business

- **Be where your customers are:** match platforms to your audience's age and habits, not to trends alone.
- **Design for mobile first:** almost all social media use is on phones.
- **Plan for video:** short-form video should be part of every content calendar.
- **Answer fast:** set up WhatsApp and inbox processes before running ads.
- **Measure results:** reach and likes matter less than messages, leads and sales.
