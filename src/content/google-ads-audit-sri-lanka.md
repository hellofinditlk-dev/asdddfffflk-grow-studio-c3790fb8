## Common Problems We Find in Sri Lankan Accounts

Across the accounts we review, the same issues come up again and again:

- **No conversion tracking, or tracking that counts the wrong thing**, such as page views counted as leads.
- **Broad match keywords without negatives**, so ads appear for job searches, free downloads or unrelated products.
- **Display network left on in Search campaigns**, spending on low-quality placements.
- **Whole-island targeting** for businesses that only serve Colombo or one district.
- **One ad group with dozens of unrelated keywords**, which lowers Quality Score and raises cost per click.
- **Landing on the homepage** instead of a page that matches the ad.
- **No call or WhatsApp assets**, even though most Sri Lankan customers prefer to call or message.

Most of these are quick to fix, and fixing them often reduces cost per lead without increasing budget. Our post on [Google Ads optimisation tips](/blog/google-ads-optimization-tips-improve-campaign-performance) covers several of these in more detail.
