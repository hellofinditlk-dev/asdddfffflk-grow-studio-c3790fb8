## Creative That Works on TikTok

TikTok rewards videos that look and feel like TikTok. Polished TV-style ads usually perform worse than simple, authentic videos.

- **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** Start with movement, a question or a surprising result.
- **Shoot vertical, on a phone.** Natural lighting and real people beat studio production.
- **Use trends wisely.** Popular sounds and formats help, but only when they fit your brand.
- **Speak your audience's language.** Sinhala, Singlish or English, whichever your customers use.
- **Show, don't tell.** Demonstrations, unboxings, before-and-after and reactions.
- **Refresh often.** TikTok creative tires quickly, so we plan new videos regularly.
