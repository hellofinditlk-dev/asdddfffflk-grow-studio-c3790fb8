# Hiring a Social Media Manager in Sri Lanka – In-House or Agency?

Every growing business reaches the point where posting "when we have time" isn't enough. You need someone responsible for content, ads, replies and results. The big question is whether to hire a social media manager in-house, use a freelancer, or work with an agency.

This guide compares the real costs, skills and trade-offs so you can choose the option that fits your business in Sri Lanka.

## What Does a Social Media Manager Do?

A social media manager or strategist is usually expected to:

- Plan a monthly content calendar
- Write captions in Sinhala, English or both
- Design posts or brief designers
- Shoot and edit Reels and TikTok videos
- Run and optimise paid ads on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok
- Reply to comments and messages
- Track results and report each month
- Keep up with platform changes and trends

That's a lot of different skills for one person, which is why many businesses find a single hire struggles to cover everything well.

## Which Option Is Right for You?

**Hire in-house if:** you post several times a day, need someone on-site daily for content, and can afford a team (not just one person).

**Hire a freelancer if:** you need help with one specific task, such as design only, and can manage the strategy yourself.

**Work with an agency if:** you want consistent content, ads and reporting without building a team, or your in-house person needs specialist support.

Many businesses use a **hybrid**: an in-house coordinator who captures daily content and handles customer replies, and an agency that plans strategy, produces design and video, and runs ads.

## Questions to Ask Before You Hire

1. Who will create Reels and videos?
2. Who will run and optimise paid ads?
3. How fast will messages and comments be answered?
4. What will monthly reports include?
5. What happens if the person leaves or is unavailable?
6. Who owns the accounts, logins and content?
