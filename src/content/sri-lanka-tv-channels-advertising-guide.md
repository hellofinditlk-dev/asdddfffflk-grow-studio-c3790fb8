## Which Channel Should You Advertise On?

There's no single "best" channel. It depends on:

- **Audience language:** Sinhala, Tamil or English speakers.
- **Age and lifestyle:** teledrama audiences, news viewers, sports fans and young viewers watch different channels and programmes.
- **Region:** some channels are stronger in certain regions.
- **Programme, not just channel:** advertising inside the right programme often matters more than the channel itself.
- **Budget:** prime-time spots on leading channels cost much more.

## Prime Time in Sri Lanka

Prime time is generally the evening block, around the main news bulletins and popular teledramas, when the largest audiences watch. Rates are highest during these slots. [CONFIRM typical prime-time hours by channel.]

Off-peak slots, such as morning and late-night, cost less and can work well for frequency, specific audiences or limited budgets.

## TV Ad Formats

| Format | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| TV commercial (TVC) spots | 15, 30 or 60-second ads in breaks | Brand awareness, launches, promotions |
| Programme sponsorship | Brand mentions and logos around a show | Long-term brand association |
| Scrollers and tickers | Text running across the screen | Announcements, offers, contact numbers |
| Squeeze-backs / L-bands | Ads shown beside programme content | Visibility without a full break |
| Product placement | Products shown inside programmes | Natural brand exposure |

## How to Plan a TV Campaign in Sri Lanka

1. **Define the audience and goal.** Awareness, a launch, or a short promotion.
2. **Choose channels and programmes** that match the audience.
3. **Decide the mix:** prime time for reach, off-peak for frequency.
4. **Produce the TVC.** Quality matters on TV; see our [video production in Sri Lanka](/video-production-sri-lanka) service.
5. **Book the schedule** and get confirmations from each channel.
6. **Monitor transmission** to confirm ads aired as booked.
7. **Measure impact** through enquiries, website visits, search interest and sales.

### Is TV advertising still worth it in Sri Lanka?
